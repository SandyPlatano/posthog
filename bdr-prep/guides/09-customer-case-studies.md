## Detailed Summaries

### Y Combinator
**World's top startup accelerator (Dropbox, Airbnb, Reddit alumni)**

- **Use Case**: Startup School, YC Library, Co-Founder Matching ("dating app for co-founders")
- **Result**: 40% more messages sent, 35% more requests accepted
- **How**: A/B tested engagement strategies with PostHog Experiments
- **Note**: 65% of every YC batch uses PostHog

---

### Supabase
**Open-source Firebase alternative**

- **Use Case**: Tracking acquisition channels and identifying partnership opportunities
- **Result**: 10X weekly user acquisition
- **How**: Spotted new AI builder tools appearing, made strategic partnership decisions
- **Key Insight**: Uses cohort analysis to understand user segments

---

### ElevenLabs
**Leading AI voice generation company (founded 2022)**

- **Use Case**: Full product development lifecycle
- **Quote**: "We use basically everything in PostHog"
- **How**: Feature flags for staged rollouts, cohort targeting, weekly retention tracking
- **Key Insight**: Monitors TestFlight retention for mobile app development

---

### Hasura
**Open-source GraphQL engine (used by Airbus, Atlassian, Netlify)**

- **Use Case**: Conversion funnel optimization
- **Result**: 10-20% conversion rate improvement
- **Why PostHog**: SQL access to raw data for custom analyses impossible with traditional tools

---

### Phantom
**Solana crypto wallet (Chrome extension)**

- **Use Case**: Performance monitoring and reliability tracking
- **Result**: 90% reduction in failure rates
- **How**: Tracked failure rates in PostHog, identified RPC provider issues, switched providers
- **Key Insight**: PostHog helped make critical infrastructure decisions

---

### Vendasta
