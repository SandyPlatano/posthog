## Role Overview

### What is a Founding BDR at PostHog?

As Founding BDR, you'll build and scale outbound sales efforts, engage potential clients with creative outreach, and develop effective strategies. This is a unique opportunity because:

- **First of its kind**: PostHog has been primarily inbound-focused; you'll pioneer their outbound motion
- **Technical requirements**: You need to carry sales conversations AND possess technical chops to talk to engineers
- **Autonomy**: PostHog values people who can own problems end-to-end without hand-holding

### Key Requirements

1. **Technical aptitude** - Be the primary person responsible for customer relationships, including solving technical problems
2. **Great interpersonal skills** - Customers should be excited to work with you
3. **Genuine passion for helping customers succeed**
4. **Prioritization skills** - Work with prospects at different states, prioritize accordingly
5. **Motivated by revenue growth**

### What PostHog Does NOT Want

PostHog explicitly states they don't want someone who will:
- Take cold leads just to run MEDPICC from their car phone
- Promise 50% discounts to close deals before the next full moon
- Execute their previous company's playbook

They're doing things differently from 90% of the industry.

## Target: $20k+ Annual Customers

The sales team focuses on customers who are paying or could pay **$20k+/year**. Key thresholds:

- **$20k**: Minimum for contract customization, BAA customization, and 20% prepaid discount
- **$60k**: Additional 5% discount on prepaid credits
- **$100k**: Additional 15% discount on prepaid credits

## Learning Path Timeline

### Week 1: Foundation
- Complete all priority guides in this repo
- Set up a PostHog account and implement on a test project
- Read the PostHog handbook cover-to-cover

### Week 2: Technical Depth
- Implement PostHog in 3 different frameworks (React, Next.js, Python)
- Set up feature flags and run a test experiment
- Configure session replay and understand privacy controls

### Week 3: Sales Preparation
- Study ICP scoring and qualification criteria
- Practice the "no BS" sales approach
- Prepare technical demos for common use cases

### Week 4: Interview Preparation
- Mock outbound campaigns to PostHog's ICP
- Prepare for technical questions about implementation
- Understand competitive differentiation cold

## Key Differentiators to Remember

1. **Open Source**: Full transparency, can self-host, community trust
2. **All-in-One**: Replaces Heap + LaunchDarkly + Hotjar
3. **Autocapture**: Starts collecting data immediately, no engineering required
4. **Engineer-First**: Built for product engineers, not marketers
5. **Transparent Pricing**: Self-serve even for Enterprise, no "talk to sales" wall
6. **Native Integration**: Jump from analytics to session replay to understand "why"

## Success Metrics for Your Preparation

- [ ] Can explain PostHog's value prop in 30 seconds
- [ ] Can implement PostHog in a web app without documentation
- [ ] Can qualify a lead using PostHog's ICP criteria
- [ ] Can differentiate PostHog from Amplitude, Mixpanel, and Heap
- [ ] Can demo product analytics, session replay, and feature flags
- [ ] Understand the $20k contract threshold and discount structure
- [ ] Know why PostHog doesn't do traditional enterprise sales
- [ ] Can articulate PostHog's culture and values authentically
