# 🦔 The PostHog Outbound Adventure
## A Non-Sleazy Sales Game Presentation

---

## 🎮 PRESENTATION OVERVIEW

**Title:** The PostHog Outbound Adventure: Protect the Brand, Build the Pipeline

**Format:** Linear game narrative presentation (single path, no branching)

**Goal:** Present a complete outbound strategy that respects PostHog's brand while creating real value for potential customers

**Duration:** ~15-20 minute presentation

**Structure:** 7 Acts with 5 characters guiding the journey

---

## 🎭 MEET YOUR CHARACTERS

### **HEDGE** (The PostHog Guide) 🦔
- **Role:** Your wise mentor and the voice of PostHog values
- **Personality:** Direct, honest, slightly irreverent, technical
- **Voice:** "Look, here's the deal..." / "Let's not BS ourselves..." / "Ship it or forget it"
- **Catchphrase:** "Don't tiptoe. Just be honest."

### **SPIKE** (The Sleazy Sales Snake) 🐍
- **Role:** The bad example - shows what NOT to do
- **Personality:** Smooth-talking, promises everything, gatekeeps, pushes hard
- **Voice:** "Just tell them whatever they want to hear!" / "Always. Be. Closing!"
- **Purpose:** Represents everything PostHog stands against

### **PIPPA** (Product Engineer Penguin) 🐧
- **Role:** Your ideal customer - Head of Product at a B2B SaaS company
- **Personality:** Smart, skeptical, hates BS, values transparency
- **Pain Points:** Using 5 different tools, data silos, complex integrations, paying $3K/month
- **What she values:** Honesty, technical depth, trying before buying

### **STACK** (The Tool Advisor) 🦫
- **Role:** Technical advisor who helps choose the right tech stack
- **Personality:** Pragmatic, "use the right tool for the job"
- **Voice:** "What problem are we actually solving here?"

### **DATA** (The Research Owl) 🦉
- **Role:** Helps with research and data sourcing at scale
- **Personality:** Methodical, detail-oriented, always qualifies assumptions
- **Voice:** "Based on third-party signals..." / "Not sure, possibly, is this...?"
- **Catchphrase:** "Never assume. Always qualify."

## THE POSTHOG OUTBOUND PRINCIPLES

### 1. VALUE BEFORE EXTRACTION
```
Give before you ask
Make it genuinely useful, not a thinly-veiled pitch
Contribution > Extraction
```

### 2. UNASSUMPTIVE TONE
```
You have 3rd party data, not mind-reading powers
Qualify your statements: "Not sure..." "Possibly..." "Is this...?"
Never assume you know their business
```

### 3. HONEST OVER CLEVER
```
Acknowledge when you're not a fit
Respect "no" without trying to "handle" it
Be transparent about tradeoffs
```

### 4. SHIP EXPERIMENTS, NOT GUESSES
```
Test multiple angles
Measure what works
Iterate fast
Treat outbound like product development
```

### 5. PROTECT THE BRAND ABOVE ALL
```
Every message represents PostHog's reputation
One sleazy tactic can undo years of trust
When in doubt, choose the more respectful approach
```
