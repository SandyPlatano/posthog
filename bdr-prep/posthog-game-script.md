# 🦔 The PostHog Outbound Adventure
## A Non-Sleazy Sales Game

---

## 🎮 GAME OVERVIEW

**Title:** The PostHog Outbound Adventure: Protect the Brand, Build the Pipeline

**Format:** Interactive choose-your-own-adventure style game where players navigate outbound sales scenarios

**Goal:** Build a sustainable outbound motion that respects PostHog's brand while creating real value for potential customers

**Win Condition:** Successfully reach qualified potential customers without being sleazy

**Lose Condition:** Damage PostHog's brand reputation with terrible sales tactics

---

## 🎭 CHARACTERS

### **HEDGE** (The PostHog Guide)
- **Role:** Your wise mentor and the voice of PostHog values
- **Personality:** Direct, honest, slightly irreverent, technical
- **Voice:** "Look, here's the deal..." / "Let's not BS ourselves..." / "Ship it or forget it"
- **Catchphrase:** "Don't tiptoe. Just be honest."

### **SPIKE** (The Sleazy Sales Snake) 🐍
- **Role:** Antagonist who tempts you with "quick wins" and traditional sales tactics
- **Personality:** Smooth-talking, promises everything, data-hoards, gatekeeps
- **Voice:** "Just tell them whatever they want to hear!" / "Gatekeeper the pricing!"
- **Catchphrase:** "Always. Be. Closing."
- **Purpose:** Represents everything PostHog stands against

### **PIPPA** (Product Engineer Penguin) 🐧
- **Role:** Your ideal customer persona
- **Personality:** Smart, skeptical, hates BS, values transparency
- **Voice:** "Show, don't tell" / "Do you actually understand my problem?"
- **Pain Points:** Using 5 different tools, data silos, complex integrations
- **What she values:** Honesty, technical depth, trying before buying

### **STACK** (The Tool Consolidator) 🦫
- **Role:** Technical advisor who helps you choose the right tech stack
- **Personality:** Pragmatic, "use the right tool for the job"
- **Voice:** "What problem are we actually solving here?"

### **DATA** (The Research Owl) 🦉
- **Role:** Helps with research and data sourcing at scale
- **Personality:** Methodical, detail-oriented, always qualifies assumptions
- **Voice:** "Based on third-party signals..." / "Not sure, possibly, is this...?"
- **Catchphrase:** "Never assume. Always qualify."
